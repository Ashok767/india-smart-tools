About India Smart Tools

India Smart Tools is a collection of simple browser-based utilities designed to help people estimate salary take-home pay, compare job offers, understand loan EMIs, calculate CGPA and attendance, and re-save PDF files.

Our calculators provide general estimates. Actual salary, tax, loan and academic outcomes depend on employer policies, applicable laws, lender terms and university rules.

We aim to keep the tools easy to use, transparent about assumptions and accessible without requiring an account.
