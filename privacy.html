The calculator inputs are processed in your browser by this static website. The site code does not send those values to an India Smart Tools server. The PDF utility reads and re-saves the selected file in your browser; it does not intentionally upload the file to our server.

Those providers may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests those resources. Review their policies before using the site.

Do not enter highly sensitive personal, financial, identity or health information into the tools. The tools are not designed to collect such information.
